# Shared Mailboxes and Calendar Delegation in Outlook

## Shared mailboxes
Shared mailboxes (for example `payables@northwind.example` or `hr-help@northwind.example`) let a team send and receive from a common address. Shared mailboxes do not have their own password and cannot be signed into directly.

### Requesting access
1. Go to https://help.northwind.example and select **Request something > Access Request > Shared Mailbox**.
2. Enter the mailbox address and choose **Full Access** (read and manage) and/or **Send As** (send mail that appears to come from the shared address).
3. The mailbox owner approves the request. IT completes it within **1 business day** of approval.
4. The mailbox appears automatically in Outlook's folder pane within **60 minutes** (auto-mapping). Restart Outlook if it does not.

### Sending from a shared mailbox
In a new email, click **From** (if hidden, enable it under **Options > Show Fields > From**), choose **Other email address**, and enter the shared address. Sent items are saved in the shared mailbox's Sent Items folder.

### Adding it manually
If the mailbox does not auto-map: **File > Account Settings > Account Settings > Change > More Settings > Advanced > Add**, then enter the mailbox name.

## Calendar delegation
You can give an assistant or colleague permission to manage your calendar yourself - no ticket needed.

1. In Outlook, go to **File > Account Settings > Delegate Access** and click **Add**.
2. Select the person and set **Calendar** to **Editor (can read, create, and modify items)**.
3. Optionally tick **Delegate receives copies of meeting-related messages sent to me**.
4. Click **OK**.

To share your calendar with view-only rights instead, go to the Calendar view, right-click your calendar, choose **Sharing Permissions**, add the person and select **Can view all details** or **Can view when I'm busy**.

Delegates open your calendar via **Calendar > Add Calendar > Open Shared Calendar**.

## When to contact IT
If a shared mailbox has not appeared after 24 hours, or you need a new shared mailbox created (manager approval required), open a ticket under **Email & Collaboration > Shared Mailbox**.
