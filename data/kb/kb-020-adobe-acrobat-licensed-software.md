# Adobe Acrobat Pro and Other Licensed Software Requests

## Overview
Some software requires a paid, named-user license. These licenses are charged to your department's cost center, so they require manager approval. Free alternatives are available for many needs.

| Software | License needed? | Annual cost to cost center | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adobe Acrobat **Reader** | No | Free | Company Portal |
| Adobe Acrobat **Pro** | Yes | $240 | Licensed Software Request |
| Adobe Creative Cloud (Photoshop, Illustrator) | Yes | $840 | Licensed Software Request + Marketing approval |
| Microsoft Visio Plan 2 | Yes | $180 | Licensed Software Request |
| Microsoft Project Plan 3 | Yes | $360 | Licensed Software Request |

## Do you really need Acrobat Pro?
Acrobat Reader (free) can view, fill in forms, comment, and add **e-signatures**. Microsoft Word can export to PDF (**File > Save As > PDF**). Acrobat Pro is needed to **edit PDF text**, combine files, redact, OCR scanned documents, or create fillable forms. For signature workflows with external parties, use **DocuSign** via Okta instead.

## Requesting a licensed application
1. Go to https://help.northwind.example and select **Request something > Software Request > Licensed Software**.
2. Choose the product, provide a business justification, and enter your department's **cost center** code.
3. Your **manager approves** the request; Creative Cloud also requires Marketing approval.
4. IT assigns the license within **2 business days** of approval.
5. For Adobe products, open **Company Portal**, install **Adobe Acrobat** (the same app is used for Reader and Pro), and sign in with your Northwind email using **Company or School Account**. The Pro features unlock automatically.

## License reclamation
Licenses that are **unused for 60 days** are automatically reclaimed and reassigned. You will receive a warning email 7 days before reclamation. Licenses are also removed when you leave the company or change roles.

## When to contact IT
If Acrobat still shows "Reader" features after your license is assigned, sign out of Acrobat (**Help > Sign Out**), sign back in, and restart. If that fails, add a comment to your request ticket on the portal.
