# Hardware Damage and Replacement (Cracked Screen, Battery, Keyboard)

## Overview
Company laptops, monitors and accessories are covered by a **Dell ProSupport** warranty with accidental damage protection for **4 years**. Report damage promptly so the device can be repaired before it gets worse.

## Reporting damage
1. Report the damage within **24 hours** by opening a ticket at https://help.northwind.example under **Hardware > Damage / Repair**.
2. Include: what happened, when, the laptop's service tag (sticker underneath), and photos of the damage.
3. Back up any files that are not in OneDrive.
4. IT will schedule an on-site Dell repair (usually within **3 business days**) or swap the device.

## Swollen or overheating battery - safety first
If the laptop case is bulging, the touchpad is lifted, the laptop is unusually hot, or you smell burning:
1. **Stop using it immediately**, shut it down and unplug the charger.
2. Do not charge it, puncture it, or put it in a bag.
3. Place it on a hard, non-flammable surface and call local **4357**. Swollen batteries are treated as **P2 (High)** priority.

## Loaner devices
If your laptop is unusable (cracked screen, broken keyboard, won't boot), you can collect a **loaner laptop the same business day** from the **IT depot on the 2nd floor** (open 8:00-17:00, Monday to Friday). Remote employees receive a loaner by courier within **2 business days**.

## Cost and accidental damage
- The **first** accidental damage repair in a 12-month period is free.
- A **second** accidental damage within 12 months requires manager approval and a **$250** charge to the department's cost center.
- Damage caused by negligence (for example leaving the laptop in a car) may require a lost/stolen review (see KB-023).
- Normal wear and battery degradation (battery health below **80%**) are covered at no cost. You can check battery health via **Dell Power Manager > Battery Information**.

## What not to do
- Do not attempt your own repairs or take the laptop to a third-party repair shop - this voids the warranty and may expose company data.
- Do not order replacement parts yourself.

## When to contact IT
Always open a ticket for any physical damage. For safety issues (battery, sparks, liquid spill on a powered-on device), call local **4357** immediately.
