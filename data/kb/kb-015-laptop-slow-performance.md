# Laptop Running Slow

## Quick Checklist (Dell Latitude 5440 / 16 GB RAM / 512 GB SSD)
1. **Reboot** (closing the lid only sleeps; reboot at least every **7 days**).
2. **Task Manager (`Ctrl+Shift+Esc`):** close memory-heavy browser tabs or unused apps. Never kill *Microsoft Defender*, *CrowdStrike*, or *Intune Management Extension*.
3. **Disk Space:** keep >= **15% free** via **Settings > System > Storage > Storage Sense** and OneDrive **Free up space** (KB-013).
4. **Power & Updates:** set **Settings > System > Power & battery > Power mode** to **Best performance** when plugged in, run **Windows Update** (KB-021), and apply driver/BIOS updates in **Dell Command | Update**.

> **4-Year Refresh Cycle:** Standard laptops are replaced every **4 years**. Managers can request an early upgrade under **Hardware > Laptop Upgrade**, or open a **Hardware > Performance** ticket with a Task Manager screenshot and service tag if slowness persists.
