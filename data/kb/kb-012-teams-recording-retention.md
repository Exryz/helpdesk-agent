# Teams Meeting Recording, Transcripts and Retention

## Who can record
Meeting organizers and presenters from Northwind Labs can record Teams meetings. Guests and external attendees cannot start recordings. Recording is disabled for meetings labeled **Restricted** (see KB-024).

## Before you record
- You **must announce** that the meeting is being recorded and obtain consent. Teams shows a banner to all participants, but a verbal notice is required by company policy, especially when external participants are present.
- Do not record HR, disciplinary or medical discussions.

## How to record
1. In the meeting, click **More (...) > Record and transcribe > Start recording**.
2. Transcription starts automatically with the recording.
3. To stop, click **More > Record and transcribe > Stop recording**. Recording also stops when everyone leaves.

## Where recordings are stored
| Meeting type | Storage location |
|---|---|
| Regular (non-channel) meeting | The **organizer's OneDrive**, in the **Recordings** folder |
| Channel meeting | The team's **SharePoint** site, in the channel's **Documents > Recordings** folder |

A link to the recording appears in the meeting chat once processing finishes, usually within **15-30 minutes** of the meeting ending.

## Retention
- Recordings **expire automatically 120 days** after they are created. The owner receives an email **14 days** before expiry.
- To keep a recording longer, open it in OneDrive or SharePoint, click **File info > Details**, and change the **Expiration date** (or choose **No expiration**). Only do this if there is a business need.
- Transcripts follow the same retention as the recording.
- Expired recordings go to the recycle bin and can be restored for **90 days**.

## Sharing recordings
Internal attendees automatically get view access. To share with someone who did not attend, use **Share** in OneDrive with **Specific people**. Sharing recordings externally follows the data classification rules in KB-024.

## When to contact IT
If a recording never appears after 24 hours, open a ticket under **Email & Collaboration > Teams** and include the meeting title, date and organizer.
